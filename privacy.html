Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy covers "Cubeline uploader", the internal tool Cubeline uses with Google APIs (YouTube Data API).

What the tool accesses

  • Cubeline's own YouTube channels, to upload Cubeline's own videos (scope: youtube.upload).
  • Public comments on Cubeline's own videos, read with a YouTube API key: the commenter's public display name, the comment text, like count and time.

How we use it

  • Videos are uploaded only to channels Cubeline owns.
  • Comments are used only to count viewer votes and to pick popular requests for the next episode. A commenter's public handle may be shown in the next episode as a "resident" or credit. Anyone can opt out by writing #anon (or #匿名) in their comment, and we will not show their name.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored only on Cubeline's own computer. Comment data is kept only as long as needed for the series (at most 90 days) and then deleted. To ask us to remove your name from a future episode or from our records, email us.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cubeline uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

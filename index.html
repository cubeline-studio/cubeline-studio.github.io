Cubeline

Cubeline is a small studio that makes short animated videos with blocky 3D characters: map battle simulations, "what if it vanished" explainers, couple comedy, and series that viewers build together through comments.

Our shows are published on YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Facebook.

Cubeline uploader

"Cubeline uploader" is an internal tool. It uploads Cubeline's own videos to Cubeline's own YouTube channels and reads public comments on those videos (for viewer votes and requests). It is used only by Cubeline and is not offered to other users.
